Visivra™ Ingredients

Visivra™ is marketed as an eye-health dietary supplement designed to support visual wellness, retinal health, and everyday eye comfort. According to the product's published ingredient list, the formula contains six key ingredients: lutein, zeaxanthin, bilberry extract, astaxanthin, zinc, and vitamin A.

1

Lutein

Lutein is a naturally occurring carotenoid found in the retina, particularly in the macula, which is responsible for central vision. It helps support normal macular pigment and protects cells against oxidative stress. Adequate lutein intake is an important part of nutritional eye health.
2

Zeaxanthin

Zeaxanthin is another carotenoid found in the macula. It works alongside lutein as part of the eye's natural pigment system and contributes to antioxidant protection. It is commonly included in eye-nutrition formulas intended to support macular health.
3

Bilberry Extract

Bilberry extract is derived from the fruit of the bilberry plant and contains anthocyanins, which are naturally occurring plant compounds. These compounds have antioxidant properties and have been investigated for their potential role in eye health. Evidence for specific vision improvements from bilberry supplements remains limited.
4

Astaxanthin

Astaxanthin is a reddish carotenoid found in certain algae and seafood. It is studied for its antioxidant properties and potential effects on eye comfort. Its inclusion in Visivra adds another source of carotenoid-based nutritional support.
5

Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in numerous bodily functions, including normal immune function and vitamin A metabolism. The retina requires zinc for several processes associated with normal vision. Adequate intake helps maintain healthy visual function, particularly when dietary intake is insufficient.
6

Vitamin A

Vitamin A is an essential nutrient that helps maintain normal vision, especially in low-light conditions. It also supports the health of the cornea and other tissues. Getting enough vitamin A is important, but excessive intake from certain supplement forms can be harmful.
